01 / SALARY CREDIT

A bracket, not your take-home pay.

Monthly salary credit (MSC) is the contribution base assigned by the SSS schedule. Regular SS is capped at ₱20,000 MSC; any excess goes to the mandatory provident fund.

02 / WHO PAYS

The right shares for your membership.

Business and household employees share contributions with their employer. Self-employed members pay EC themselves. Voluntary members and land-based OFWs have no EC.

03 / YOUR PRIVACY

Your numbers stay with you.

Calculations run in your browser using the bundled library and tables. No accounts, analytics, or external services. Only source and repository links need internet access.

A reference calculation, with a clear scope. Includes the 2023 and 2025 official table groups. Eligibility, MSC-change limits, payment deadlines, advance-payment posting, penalties and optional voluntary Pension Booster savings require separate handling. A zero-income input still matches the minimum table bracket; it does not establish an obligation to contribute.
