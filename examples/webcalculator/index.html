01 / SALARY CREDIT

A bracket, not your take-home pay.

Monthly salary credit (MSC) is the contribution base assigned by the SSS schedule. Regular SS is capped at ₱20,000 MSC; any excess goes to the mandatory provident fund.

02 / WHO PAYS

The right shares for your membership.

Business and household employees share contributions with their employer. Self-employed members pay EC themselves. Voluntary members and land-based OFWs have no EC.

03 / YOUR PRIVACY

Your numbers stay with you.

Calculations run in your browser using the bundled library and tables. No accounts, analytics, or external services. Only source and repository links need internet access.

Definition of terms

Understand the inputs and where your contribution goes. Salary credits are calculation bases; contribution amounts are what gets paid.

SSS / SS
SSS means Social Security System. SS means Social Security, the regular benefit program administered by SSS.
Monthly salary credit (MSC)
The contribution base selected from the official schedule, not your take-home pay or the amount you pay to SSS. It may differ from actual income.
Compensation / income
The earnings amount entered for the selected period. The calculator uses its monthly equivalent to find the matching contribution bracket; it does not decide which earnings legally count as compensation.
Contribution bracket
An income range in the official table with a corresponding salary credit and contribution amount.
Regular SS
The contribution component for regular SSS benefits, such as retirement, disability and sickness, subject to the applicable qualifying rules.
WISP / MPF
WISP means Workers’ Investment and Savings Program; MPF means Mandatory Provident Fund. These labels identify the mandatory provident-fund component in the bundled schedules, credited to the member’s individual account. It is included in the total, not an optional extra or the separate voluntary savings program.
EC — Employees’ Compensation
A separate contribution for work-related sickness, injury or death coverage. Employers pay it for business, sea-based OFW and household employees; self-employed members pay it themselves. The bundled voluntary, non-working-spouse and land-based OFW schedules have no EC contribution.
Salary-credit breakdown
Total salary credit is split between Regular SS and MPF. In the bundled 2023 and 2025 schedules, Regular SS uses up to ₱20,000 MSC; the excess is assigned to MPF. These are calculation bases, not additional payments.
Employee / employer / member share
Employee is the worker’s portion; employer is the employer’s portion. Member is the full amount paid by an individually paying member. Household employers pay the entire contribution when actual monthly compensation is below ₱5,000.
Total contribution
The combined contribution across all applicable components and payers. When Member and Total show the same amount, they describe one payment, not two separate charges.
Applicable month
The month the contribution is for. It selects the effective schedule; it is not necessarily the payment date or deadline.
Approved MSC
An eligible salary credit already established under SSS rules. Selecting an MSC here does not approve membership or a change in salary credit.
Pay frequency / annualized estimate
Pay frequency describes how often income is received. Semi-monthly means twice a month; biweekly means every two weeks. The sample converts these amounts to monthly equivalents; weekly and biweekly averages can differ from actual earnings in a particular month.
OFW / kasambahay
OFW means Overseas Filipino Worker. Land-based and sea-based OFWs use different schedule groups. Kasambahay means a household employee.
PHP / centavos
PHP (₱) means Philippine pesos. One peso equals 100 centavos. Library and downloaded JSON monetary fields use whole-number centavos, so 528000 means ₱5,280.00.
Worked example: ₱5,280 monthly contribution

For a self-employed member at ₱35,000 MSC under the 2025 schedule: ₱20,000 Regular SS base × 15% = ₱3,000; ₱15,000 MPF base × 15% = ₱2,250; plus ₱30 EC = ₱5,280 per month. The member pays the full ₱5,280 once.

Sources: 2025 SSS self-employed schedule and Employees’ Compensation Program. Definitions describe the bundled schedules; benefit eligibility requires the applicable SSS rules.
